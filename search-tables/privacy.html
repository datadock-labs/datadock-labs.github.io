Search Tables for Confluence – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026. Publisher: DataDock Labs ("we").

Summary

Search Tables runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform. We do not operate servers for the app, and no Confluence content or personal data is sent to us or to any third party.

What the app processes

  • Search results: when a page with the macro is viewed, the app runs the configured Confluence search as the viewing user and shows the results. Results are not stored.
  • Saved searches: when a user saves a search, the app stores its name, query settings, visibility (personal or shared) and the owner's Atlassian account ID in Forge storage, hosted by Atlassian.
  • Macro settings: stored by Confluence as part of the page.
  • CSV export: generated in the user's browser and saved to their device.

What we do not do

The app adds no analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies. We do not sell or share data. The app has no external network access (no egress).

Retention and deletion

Saved searches remain until their owner deletes them on the Search Tables page. When the app is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes the app's stored data according to its Forge data retention rules.

Atlassian

Hosting, storage and logs are provided by Atlassian under its own privacy policy.

Support requests

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer you, and delete them when no longer needed.
