How to list Confluence pages modified between two dates

Search Tables is a new Confluence Cloud app and is coming soon to the Atlassian Marketplace (it is in Atlassian's review now). The steps below describe what the app does today.

Short answer: CQL accepts dates: lastmodified >= "2026-10-01" AND lastmodified < "2026-11-01" returns the pages last changed in October. The Recently Updated macro has no date setting. In Search Tables you do not even need to type the query: choose Custom range under Last modified and pick the two dates.

The query

type = page AND space = "ENG" AND lastmodified >= "2026-10-01" AND lastmodified < "2026-11-01" ORDER BY lastmodified DESC

Use created instead of lastmodified for pages created in the period.

Step by step

  1. Edit a page, type /Search Tables and stay on the Filters tab.
  2. Pick the space under Spaces and Page under Content types.
  3. Set Last modified to Custom range and fill in From and To. Both days are included.
  4. Choose the columns Title, Last modified, Modified by and Created, then save. Press Export CSV for an audit or a monthly report.

Compared with what Confluence offers built in

  • Recently Updated: the latest changes only; no start or end date.
  • Advanced search: "last modified" offers fixed recent periods; the result cannot be kept on a page or exported.
  • Search Tables: any range, saved on the page, sortable and exportable.

FAQ

A page was edited in October and again in November. Is it in the October list? No. lastmodified is the date of the latest edit, so the page shows up in November's list. CQL has no "edited at any time during" field.

Can I make it "the last 30 days" instead of fixed dates? Yes: choose Last 30 days under Last modified; the table then moves with the calendar.

How many rows can I export? Up to 2,000 per export.
