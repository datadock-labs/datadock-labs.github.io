How to find Confluence pages that have not been updated in a year

Search Tables is a new Confluence Cloud app and is coming soon to the Atlassian Marketplace (it is in Atlassian's review now). The steps below describe what the app does today.

Short answer: CQL can do it with a relative date: lastmodified < now("-52w") returns every page whose last edit is more than 52 weeks old. The built-in macros cannot show that list (Recently Updated only shows the newest changes), so put the query in a Search Tables macro: you get a live table of stale pages, oldest first, with the last editor next to each one, and a CSV for the clean-up.

The query

Pages in one space that nobody has edited for a year, oldest first. Replace ENG with your space key.

type = page AND space = "ENG" AND lastmodified < now("-52w") ORDER BY lastmodified ASC

Other thresholds: now("-26w") for six months, now("-90d") for 90 days. Drop the space clause to check every space you can see.

Step by step

  1. Edit a page (for example your space's "Content review" page), type /Search Tables and open the CQL tab.
  2. Paste the query and choose the columns Title, Space, Last modified, Modified by and Labels.
  3. Save. The table is live: every time someone opens the page it shows the pages that are stale today, so the list shrinks as people update or archive content.
  4. Press Export CSV to hand the list to page owners or to track the review in a spreadsheet.

Compared with what Confluence offers built in

  • Recently Updated macro: shows the newest changes, not the oldest; it cannot list pages that have not changed.
  • Advanced search: the "last modified" filter offers recent periods; the result is a search screen, not a table you can keep on a page or export.
  • Content Manager and analytics: depending on your plan and permissions, admins may have a content view with last-updated dates. Search Tables works for anyone who can edit a page and shows only what the viewer can see.

FAQ

Does "last modified" count comments or views? No. It is the date of the last edit of the page itself. A page that is read every day but never edited still counts as stale.

Can I see who created the page instead? Yes, add the Created by and Created columns.

How many rows can I export? Up to 2,000 per export. For a big site, run the query per space.

Will people see pages they cannot open? No. The search runs with the viewer's permissions.
