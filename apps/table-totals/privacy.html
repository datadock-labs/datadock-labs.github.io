Table Totals & CSV Export – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026. Publisher: DataDock Labs ("we").

Table Totals & CSV Export ("the add-on") is a Google Workspace add-on for Google Docs. This policy explains what data the add-on uses.

Summary

The add-on runs on Google Apps Script inside your Google account. We do not operate servers for it, and your document content is not sent to us or to any third party.

What the add-on accesses

  • The document you have open (documents.currentonly): to read the tables in it, and to add, update or remove the summary rows the add-on creates. It cannot open any other document.
  • Files the add-on creates in your Google Drive (drive.file): when you click "Save as CSV" or "Save as TSV", the add-on creates one file in your Drive. It can only see files it created; it cannot see your other Drive files.

What the add-on stores

  • Settings: your choices (which statistics, header option, label language, number format) are kept in Google's per-user script storage so the card remembers them. No document content is stored.
  • Exported files: stay in your own Google Drive. You can delete them at any time.
  • Error log: if the add-on hits an error, Google Apps Script records the error message (for example "Table 2 was not found") in the add-on's Google Cloud log, which we can read. The add-on's own error messages contain no document content.

What we do not do

The add-on makes no network requests outside Google and adds no analytics, tracking or advertising. We do not sell, share or transfer Google user data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The add-on's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove the add-on and its access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Support requests

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer you, and delete them when no longer needed.
