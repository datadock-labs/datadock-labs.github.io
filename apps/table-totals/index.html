Table Totals & CSV Export

Totals for tables in Google Docs™, and a one-click CSV export. A free Google Workspace add-on.

Totals for any table

  • Add summary rows: Total, Average, Count, Min and Max for every numeric column of the table at your cursor (or pick a table from the list).
  • Recalculate after you edit: the add-on updates the rows it created, so totals are never counted twice.
  • Reads numbers the way people type them: 1,234.56 · 1.234,56 · 1 234,56 · 1,23,456 · currencies · percentages · negatives. Results use the column's own format.
  • Header rows are detected automatically. Text, empty cells and "N/A" are skipped. Merged cells are handled.
  • Row labels in English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, Português, Türkçe and हिन्दी.

Export a table

  • Save as CSV (UTF-8, opens correctly in Excel and Sheets) or TSV, saved to your own Google Drive.
  • Cells that look like spreadsheet formulas are neutralised in the exported file.
  • Open as Google Sheet: creates a new spreadsheet in your Drive with the table, one cell per cell.

Private by design

  • Works only on the document you have open; it cannot see your other documents.
  • Exported files go to your own Google Drive; the add-on can only see files it created.
  • No servers, no tracking, nothing sent outside Google. Details: Privacy Policy.

How to use

  1. Open a document with a table and open Table Totals & CSV Export from the right side panel.
  2. Click inside a table and press Refresh (or pick a table from the list).
  3. Choose the summary rows and press Add / recalculate. Press it again after you change the table.
  4. To export, press Save as CSV, Save as TSV or Open as Google Sheet.

Limits

  • Totals are not live: click "Add / recalculate" after you change the table.
  • Not available in the Docs mobile apps (Google's limit for add-ons).
  • Dates and durations are not summed.
  • Tables of up to 10,000 cells (for example 500 rows × 20 columns). For a larger table the add-on changes nothing and says so.

FAQ

Does it change my data? It only adds, updates or removes the summary rows it created. Your other rows are not changed.

Why are totals not updated automatically? Docs does not let add-ons react to edits; click "Add / recalculate".

My numbers use a comma for decimals. The add-on detects it from the column; for ambiguous values like 1,250 choose "1.234,56" in "When a number is ambiguous".

Where is my CSV? In your Google Drive, named after the document and table (for example budget-table-2-2026-11-17.csv). The card also gives a Download link.

How big can a table be? Up to 10,000 cells. A 200-row, 20-column table takes about 2 seconds. For a larger table the add-on changes nothing and shows a message; split the table first.

What does it cost? It is free.
