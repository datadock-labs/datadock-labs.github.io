Card Score Pad – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026. Publisher: DataDock Labs. Contact: datadocklabs@gmail.com.

Summary

Card Score Pad is an offline score keeper for card and dice games. It has no account system, no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics, and we run no servers. The games, player names and statistics you enter stay on your device. The app does not connect to the internet and asks for no permissions. We do not collect, receive or share any personal data.

Data that stays on your device

Player and team names, games, rounds, settings and statistics are stored in the app's private storage on your device. We do not collect them and cannot see them.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this app data (games and settings) in your device backup or device-to-device transfer. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings; you can turn it off in Android settings.

Sharing a result

When you tap "Share" on a finished game, the app hands a plain-text summary (game, player names and scores) to your phone's share menu. Only you choose where it goes; the app sends nothing by itself.

What we do not do

No ads and no advertising SDK, no in-app purchases, no account, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, no network access, no location, contacts, photos or file permissions, no sale of personal data. The app is not directed at children.

Your choices and requests

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to delete or export; uninstalling the app removes all data it stored on your device. Questions: datadocklabs@gmail.com.

Changes

If a future version adds ads, purchases or any other data processing, this policy will be updated on this page, with a new date, before that version is released.
