Card Score Pad – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026. Publisher: DataDock Labs. Contact: datadocklabs@gmail.com.

Summary

Card Score Pad is an offline score keeper for card and dice games. It has no account system and we run no servers: the games, player names and statistics you enter stay on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google's advertising software collects some device data, described below. We never see or receive that data.

Data that stays on your device

Player and team names, games, rounds, settings and statistics are stored in the app's private storage on your device. We do not collect them. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this app data in your device backup, which is handled by Google under your Google account settings. You can turn this off in Android settings. Pro users can also export a backup file to a place they choose; that file is created only when you ask for it.

Advertising (free version)

The free version uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show a banner on the score table and, at most occasionally, a full-screen ad after a finished game. Google's SDK automatically collects and shares the following for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, encrypted in transit:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • app interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views;
  • diagnostic information about app and SDK performance;
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Android settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions)

Where required by law, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.

Purchases (Pro)

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase that removes ads and unlocks extra features. Payments are handled by Google Play under Google's terms; we do not receive your payment details. The app only checks with Google Play on your device whether Pro has been purchased. When Pro is active, the app does not request ads.

What we do not do

No account, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own, no location permission, no access to contacts, photos or files, no sale of personal data. The app is not directed at children.

Your choices and requests

Because we hold no personal data on servers, there is nothing for us to delete or export; uninstalling the app removes all data stored by it on your device. For data collected by Google's advertising SDK, use the Android ad settings above or Google's own tools. Questions: datadocklabs@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
