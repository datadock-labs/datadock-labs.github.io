Tidy Screenshot – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026. Publisher: DataDock Labs.

Summary

Tidy Screenshot does not collect, sell or share any personal data. It has no account system, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sends your screenshots or browsing data to any server.

What the extension does

When you click the toolbar button or use the keyboard shortcut, the extension captures the tab you are looking at. To produce a clean image it temporarily hides cookie banners, chat bubbles and empty ad slots with CSS and scrolls the page. All changes to the page are undone right after the capture.

Where your data goes

The captured images stay in your browser. The last five captures are kept in the extension's local storage (IndexedDB) so the result page can display them, and older ones are deleted automatically. Your three settings, your preferred result view (close-up or whole image), the number of captures you have taken and whether you have answered the one-time rating request are saved with the browser's own storage on your device; they are never sent anywhere. Files you download are saved by your browser to your computer.

Area selection and editing

From version 0.1.5 you can select an area of the page and edit the result (crop, pen, arrow, box, highlight, blur, text). The selection and every edit are processed inside your browser; nothing is uploaded. Blurred areas are pixelated in the exported file, so the original content cannot be recovered from it.

Welcome and removal pages

From version 0.1.3, the extension opens a welcome page on our website once, right after you install it, and asks your browser to open a short feedback page on our website if you remove it. Both pages are plain web pages: no scripts, cookies, analytics or forms. The feedback page only offers email links; nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. Like any website, our host (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Permissions

  • activeTab: capture only the tab you choose, only when you click or press the shortcut.
  • scripting: temporarily hide clutter and scroll the page during the capture.
  • storage: remember your settings, your preferred result view and the capture count used for the one-time rating request.
