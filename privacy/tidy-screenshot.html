Tidy Screenshot – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026. Publisher: DataDock Labs.

Summary

Tidy Screenshot does not collect, sell or share any personal data. It has no account system, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sends your screenshots or browsing data to any server.

What the extension does

When you click the toolbar button or use the keyboard shortcut, the extension captures the tab you are looking at. To produce a clean image it temporarily hides cookie banners, chat bubbles and empty ad slots with CSS and scrolls the page. All changes to the page are undone right after the capture.

Where your data goes

The captured images stay in your browser. The last five captures are kept in the extension's local storage (IndexedDB) so the result page can display them, and older ones are deleted automatically. Your three settings, the number of captures you have taken and whether you have answered the one-time rating request are saved with the browser's own storage on your device; they are never sent anywhere. Files you download are saved by your browser to your computer.

Permissions

  • activeTab: capture only the tab you choose, only when you click or press the shortcut.
  • scripting: temporarily hide clutter and scroll the page during the capture.
  • storage: remember your settings and the capture count used for the one-time rating request.
